If your browser warns about the download

Edge or Chrome may say the file "isn't commonly downloaded". That is only because the game is new and free. It is safe to keep.

In Edge

  1. 1Hover the download, click the three dots (...), then Keep.
  2. 2Click Show more, then Keep anyway.

In Chrome

  1. 1Click Keep. Newer Chrome may say Download suspicious file or Download anyway instead.

On a Mac, Safari needs no step.

The first time you open it

On Windows

Windows may stop the game once with "Windows protected your PC".

  1. 1Click More info.
  2. 2Click Run anyway.

On a Mac

macOS may say it can't verify the game. Do this once.

  1. 1Click Done on the message.
  2. 2Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security. Near the bottom, click Open Anyway.
  3. 3Click Open Anyway again and enter your Mac password. From then on it opens like any app.

Why the warning? Pokémon AEGIS is free and isn't signed with a paid certificate, so Windows and macOS ask you once before it opens.

Where your saves live

On Windows and Mac each save slot is one small file, save-1.json to save-3.json. Copy them to back up your game or to carry it to another computer.

Windows, installed%APPDATA%\Pokemon AEGIS\saves
Windows, zipThe saves folder next to Pokemon AEGIS.exe
Mac~/Library/Application Support/Pokemon AEGIS/saves
iPhoneYour saves are not files here: they stay inside the AEGIS app on your home screen. Removing it from your home screen removes them.
